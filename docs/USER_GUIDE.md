# 📚 User Guide - AI for Students Platform

Welcome to the AI for Students platform! This guide will help you get started and make the most of all the features available.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Click on **"Sign Up"** from the landing page
2. Enter your email address and create a password
3. Complete your profile information
4. You're ready to start learning!

### Signing In

1. Click on **"Sign In"** from the landing page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **"Sign In"** to access your dashboard

## 🎓 Learning Modules

### Accessing Learning Content

1. Navigate to **"Learn AI"** from the sidebar or dashboard
2. Select a level (Level 1, 2, or 3)
3. Choose a lesson to begin

### Completing Lessons

1. Read through each section of the lesson
2. Use **"Previous"** and **"Next"** buttons to navigate
3. Complete the quiz at the end (if available)
4. Click **"Mark as Complete ✓"** when finished

### Progress Tracking

- Your progress is automatically saved
- View your completed lessons in your **Profile**
- Track your learning streak and achievements

## 🎮 Interactive Games

### Available Games

1. **Train the AI** - Teach AI by providing examples
2. **AI or Human?** - Guess if content was created by AI or humans
3. **Prediction Game** - Predict AI behavior patterns
4. **Memory Match** - Match AI terms with definitions
5. **Puzzle Challenge** - Solve AI-related puzzles
6. **Quick Brain Test** - Test your AI knowledge with timed questions

### Playing Games

1. Go to **"Games"** from the sidebar
2. Select a game you want to play
3. Read the instructions
4. Click **"Start Game"** to begin
5. Complete the game to earn points and achievements

### Game Features

- **Score Tracking**: Earn points for correct answers
- **Time Tracking**: See how long you take to complete
- **Progress Saving**: Your high scores are saved automatically
- **Difficulty Levels**: Some games offer multiple difficulty levels

## 🛠️ AI Tools

### Text Summarizer

1. Navigate to **"AI Tools"** → **"Text Summarizer"**
2. Paste or type the text you want to summarize
3. Choose summary length (Short, Medium, Long)
4. Click **"Generate Summary"**
5. Copy the summary using the **"Copy"** button

**Tips:**
- Longer texts produce better summaries
- Choose the length based on your needs
- Use for articles, documents, or study materials

### Flashcard Generator

1. Go to **"AI Tools"** → **"Flashcard Generator"**
2. Enter the topic or content
3. Specify the number of flashcards needed
4. Click **"Generate Flashcards"**
5. Review and save flashcards to your profile

**Tips:**
- Be specific about the topic
- Review generated flashcards for accuracy
- Save important flashcards for later review

### Study Plan Generator

1. Navigate to **"AI Tools"** → **"Study Plan Generator"**
2. Enter your subject and goals
3. Set the duration and start date
4. Click **"Generate Study Plan"**
5. Follow the personalized plan

**Tips:**
- Set realistic goals
- Adjust the plan as needed
- Track your progress regularly

## 💬 AI Tutor

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I track my progress?

Your progress is automatically tracked. Visit your **Profile** page to see detailed statistics.

### Can I reset my progress?

Currently, progress cannot be reset. Contact support if you need assistance.

### Are the games timed?

Some games have timers, while others don't. Check individual game instructions.

### Can I use AI tools offline?

No, AI tools require an internet connection to function.

### How do I save flashcards?

Flashcards are automatically saved when generated. Access them from your **Profile**.
